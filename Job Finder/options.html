Settings

Job search (SerpApi)

Job results come from Google Jobs through SerpApi. Their free plan includes a monthly allowance of searches; each Search or Load more uses one.

Resume tailoring (Claude)

"Tailor resume" uses Claude Code from VS Code, with your Claude Code login. No API key needed.

Saving

Each tailored resume is saved with its job description and details in its own subfolder: Resume - Company - Role.md, Resume - Company - Role.html, Resume - Company - Role.pdf, job-description.md, job.json and analysis.json. Resumes you mark Ready to apply go in a Ready folder; the rest stay in Not ready.
